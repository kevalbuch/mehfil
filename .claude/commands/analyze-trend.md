# /analyze-trend

Analyze a sound's trajectory across India's regional scenes, evaluate momentum signals, and recommend lifecycle transitions or "Sound Twins".

## Purpose
Prevent catalog obsolescence by monitoring whether a sound is transitioning from Early to Rising, Peaking, or Saturated, and identifying fresher, less-saturated alternatives.

## Lifecycle Transition Framework

| Stage | Cultural Signal | Creator Recommendation | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Early** | College campus / niche scene discovery; low edit count (< 5K) | "Use now before it breaks out" | Tag as Early; monitor weekly |
| **Rising** | Rapid adoption across creators; high save velocity | "Peak creative window; ideal post timing" | Feature in Post Match priority |
| **Peaking** | National creator adoption; high reach, rising cliché risk | "Use only with a subversive/fresh angle" | Warn creators; link Sound Twins |
| **Saturated** | Repetitive brand/influencer usage; audience fatigue | "Overplayed; try this Sound Twin instead" | Demote in match; display Twin |
| **Retro Comeback** | Classic track (10+ yrs old) revived in modern aesthetics | "Nostalgic resonance; contrast with modern visuals" | Tag as Retro Comeback |

## Evaluation Checklist
1. **Scene Migration**: Has the sound traveled beyond its language origin (e.g. Punjabi track used in South Indian wedding edits, Tamil indie used in Mumbai street style)?
2. **Velocity vs. Fatigue**: Are creators still innovating with it, or has it devolved into generic point-and-dance?
3. **Sound Twin Pairing**: If the track is Peaking or Saturated, identify 2 underground tracks with:
   - Matching BPM / rhythmic groove
   - Parallel emotional temperature (e.g. melancholic longing, high-energy triumph, soft cinematic)
   - Under 50k reel appearances
