# /audit-rights

Audit Mehfil's catalog for compliance with platform policies, data ethics, and music rights rules.

## Policy Rules (Product Brief Section 7)
1. **No Hosted Binary Audio**: Mehfil does NOT host, stream, or redistribute full copyrighted audio files.
2. **Official Outbound Destinations**: Playback links must direct to approved external streaming destinations (e.g. Spotify search, YouTube, Instagram Audio).
3. **Attribution Integrity**: Every track must explicitly credit:
   - Primary artist(s) & producer
   - Language & regional scene
   - Album / Film / Label origin where applicable
4. **Correction Mechanism**: Users and artists must have a clear path to report outdated trend stages, incorrect credits, or misattributed scenes.

## Audit Checklist
- [ ] No audio files (.mp3, .wav, .m4a) in public repository.
- [ ] All `outboundUrl` links open verified query targets.
- [ ] All artists and titles are spelled accurately according to official releases.
- [ ] Catalog includes disclaimer: "Curated, not scraped. Mehfil directs listeners to official music streaming destinations."
