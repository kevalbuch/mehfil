# /generate-editorial

Curate and update the daily editorial drop on the "Today" surface of Mehfil.

## Purpose
Section 5 of the product brief requires an editorial daily selection composed of 5 distinct cultural sound archetypes:
1. **Use Now**: A rising track in its golden creative window before widespread adoption.
2. **Rising**: Fast-accelerating track jumping from campus or indie niche into broader relevance.
3. **Retro Return**: A vintage gem resurfacing with new aesthetic meaning for Gen Z.
4. **Independent Find**: A breakout track from an independent regional artist outside mainstream label rotation.
5. **Saturated Alternative**: A spotlight on a heavily overplayed sound paired directly with its fresh **Sound Twin**.

## Curation Standards
- **Linguistic diversity**: Minimum 3 different languages/scenes across the 5 daily picks.
- **Hook precision**: Verified 10-15s segment with clear audio cue.
- **Editorial commentary**: Contextual note on why this track is moving today (e.g., "Trending in Bengaluru design studios", "Breaking through Chandigarh college edits").
