# Trend Stage Taxonomy Skill

## Overview
Mehfil does not rely on noisy, unverified scraping. Instead, it provides clear, transparent cultural signals through five distinct lifecycle stages and pairs peaking/saturated tracks with underground "Sound Twins".

## The Five Trend Stages

```
   [ Early ] ──> [ Rising ] ──> [ Peaking ] ──> [ Saturated ]
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                           [ Retro Comeback ]
                                             (after years)
```

### 1. Early
- **Definition**: Heard primarily in underground scenes, independent artist releases, regional college edits, or localized subcultures.
- **Creator Signal**: Maximum creative freedom. Using this sound gives creators early-taster prestige.
- **Badge Color**: Sage / Moss Green (`#517354`)

### 2. Rising
- **Definition**: Velocity is visibly accelerating. Influencers and active creators are beginning to adopt it; search volume is spiking.
- **Creator Signal**: Optimal posting window. Algorithms respond well to emerging momentum before audience fatigue sets in.
- **Badge Color**: Terracotta / Rust (`#b65b4e`)

### 3. Peaking
- **Definition**: Widespread adoption across mainstream social platforms. Millions of views, ubiquitous on FYPs.
- **Creator Signal**: High reach but high danger of looking generic. Requires a subversive, counter-intuitive execution idea.
- **Badge Color**: Warm Amber (`#9e6925`)

### 4. Saturated
- **Definition**: Overused by brands, retail ads, and repetitive trend formats. High skip rates among discerning viewers.
- **Creator Signal**: Do NOT use for standard posts unless deliberately ironic.
- **Action**: Always present a **Sound Twin**—a fresher track with the same emotional pulse.
- **Badge Color**: Slate Taupe (`#846b63`)

### 5. Retro Comeback
- **Definition**: A vintage track (5-20+ years old) from Bollywood golden eras, regional cinema classics, or 90s indie revived through modern edits.
- **Creator Signal**: Evokes warm nostalgia, timeless comfort, or ironic juxtaposition.
- **Badge Color**: Dusty Lavender (`#66518b`)

## The Sound Twin Matching Algorithm
When a sound is **Peaking** or **Saturated**, recommend a Sound Twin:
1. **Rhythmic Cadence**: Same BPM range (± 8 BPM).
2. **Instrumentation**: Shared sonic texture (e.g. acoustic nylon strings, distorted 808s, saxophone leads).
3. **Emotional Resonance**: Matches the feeling (e.g., bittersweet yearning, victorious confidence, lazy Sunday afternoon).
4. **Freshness Criterion**: The Sound Twin must currently be in **Early** or **Rising** stage.
