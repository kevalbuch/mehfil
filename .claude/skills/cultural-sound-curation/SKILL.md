# Cultural Sound Curation Skill

## Overview
This skill provides the domain framework for discovering, contextualizing, and curating emerging sound culture across India. Mehfil's promise is: *"No language is framed as secondary; every scene can be the source of the next national moment."*

## Scene Architecture

### 1. Punjabi Indie & Street Culture
- **Key Hubs**: Chandigarh, Mohali, Ludhiana, Brampton diaspora, Doaba region.
- **Aesthetic**: Brass horns, raw acoustic tumbi, UK drill beats, retro tape flutter.
- **Creator Context**: Car windshield transitions, golden hour portraits, night highway edits, swagger and statement fits.

### 2. Tamil Indie & Alternative Wave
- **Key Hubs**: Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Bengaluru diaspora.
- **Aesthetic**: Carnatic vocal runs fused with dream-pop, synth-funk, Chennai acoustic guitars, poignant spoken hooks.
- **Creator Context**: Rainy café transitions, inside-joke carousels, Chennai sunset drives, collegiate soft launches.

### 3. Malayalam Lo-Fi & Melodic Movement
- **Key Hubs**: Kochi, Kozhikode, Thiruvananthapuram.
- **Aesthetic**: Lush acoustic fingerpicking, rain ambience, introspective melodies, breezy island chords.
- **Creator Context**: Monsoon train journeys, tea-estate walks, nostalgic friendship dumps, quiet unposed candids.

### 4. Hindi Indie & Bedroom Pop
- **Key Hubs**: Delhi NCR, Mumbai sub-circuits, Pune, Jaipur.
- **Aesthetic**: Intimate nylon-string guitars, vulnerable songwriting, analog synthesizer pads, lo-fi drum beats.
- **Creator Context**: Sunset rooftop outfits, late-night introspective thoughts, soft-launch polaroids, college campus walks.

### 5. Telugu Indie & Folk Fusion
- **Key Hubs**: Hyderabad, Vizag, Guntur.
- **Aesthetic**: High-groove percussion, acoustic melodies, folk rhythms mixed with urban soul.
- **Creator Context**: Festive gatherings, energetic café hops, dramatic arrivals, nostalgic hometown visits.

### 6. Bengali Indie & Rabindra/Folk Revivals
- **Key Hubs**: Kolkata, Shantiniketan.
- **Aesthetic**: Poetic lyricism, dotara strings, introspective acoustic textures, coffeehouse nostalgia.
- **Creator Context**: Book cafe aesthetics, Durga Puja prep, old Calcutta architecture, monsoon tram edits.

### 7. Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Assamese & Regional Frontiers
- **Marathi**: High-energy dhol-tasha, soulful contemporary ghazals, Pune alternative rock.
- **Gujarati**: Garba-synth fusions, Charotar folk-pop, Ahmedabad heritage photowalks.
- **Kannada**: Mysore acoustic folk, Bengaluru electronic indie, breezy road trip anthems.
- **Assamese**: Bihu grooves fused with indie ambient, Brahmaputra river sunsets, Northeast mist aesthetic.

## Curatorial Rules
- Always listen for the **hook within the hook**: what 12 seconds make someone pause scrolling?
- Maintain linguistic parity: never group non-Hindi tracks into an "other" or "regional" bin. Every scene gets its specific identity.
- Verify phonetic pronunciation and exact spelling of regional artist names.
