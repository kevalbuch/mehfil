# Creator Concept Engine Skill

## Overview
Mehfil is not just a chart; it is a **creator decision layer**. It shortens the journey from *"I want to post"* to *"I have the right sound and a stronger idea."*

## What Makes a Good "Fresh Angle"?
A fresh angle must NEVER be generic advice like "film yourself smiling" or "show your outfit." It must provide a specific visual editing instruction that leverages the audio's structure.

### 1. Concrete Visual Directives
- **Subversion of Expectation**: Instead of showing the bride at the beat drop, show the grandmother’s tearful laugh.
- **Atmospheric Pacing**: Show the empty room, the tea cup, the rainy window before the human subject enters.
- **Cut Constraints**: Specify exact cut counts (e.g. "Three cuts only: before, in-between, the look. Don't rush the last frame.").

### 2. Matching Post Formats to Sounds

| Reel Format | Sound Texture | Timing & Hook Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **Outfit Reveal** | Crisp snare / subtle beat drop | 1-2 seconds of detail in shadow; snap reveal on the beat drop. |
| **Travel Dump** | Flowing acoustic guitars, breezy strings | Ambient intro (wind/train) fading into vocal chorus at 0:15. |
| **Soft Launch** | Understated, warm lo-fi / indie | Blurred periphery; single intentional hand or coffee mug in frame. |
| **Wedding / Sangeet** | Soulful sufi, shehnai-acoustic fusion | Candid unposed frames; slow-motion reaction shots. |
| **College Farewell** | Bittersweet indie rock, nostalgic choir | Empty campus spots (canteen, back bench, gate) before faces. |
| **Gym / PR Edit** | Heavy 808s, dhol cadence, brass hooks | Chalk dust drop on the beat build; lift initiation right at drop. |
| **Inside Joke / Meme** | Abrupt pause, deadpan lyrical hook | Visual freeze-frame or zoom-in during lyrical silence. |

### 3. Timestamp Precision
- The hook MUST be between 10 and 15 seconds long.
- Example: `0:32 — 0:47`
- Specify what happens at the start and end of that window (vocal entry to final sustained chord).
